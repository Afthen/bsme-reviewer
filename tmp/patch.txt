Two great catches:
1. **Why `Choose quiz sets` didn't work:** Clicking it opened the dropdown, then the click bubbled up to `document`'s outside-click listener, which immediately closed it again! Adding `e.stopPropagation()` fixes both `Choose quiz sets` and `Choose topics`.
2. **Tucking `Export / Import` into `☰ Mastery`:** Moving `💾 Export` and `📥 Import` into the bottom of `#sidebar-drawer` cleans up the header and makes it 100% clear those buttons are for your **Mastery Profile** (not quiz uploads).
